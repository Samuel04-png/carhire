# Dots Car Hire — Marketing Handoff Pack

## Department Brief
- **Goal:** Turn real product screenshots into a polished client-story/case-study style social post.
- **Target audience:** Business owners and operations-heavy SMEs in Zambia/Africa that still run bookings, customer communication, fleet coordination, and reporting manually.
- **Pain point:** Scattered bookings, weak online trust, low operational visibility, and admin work spread across calls, WhatsApp, and spreadsheets.
- **Solution angle:** Byte & Berry built a cleaner digital experience for car hire operations — customer-side booking + internal admin control.
- **Recommended formats:**
  1. Instagram/Facebook carousel
  2. LinkedIn case-study post
  3. Static proof post using one hero shot + supporting device mockups
